# Welcome to my world

I'm Tim,

a passionate **hobby photographer** and **biology student**. Over the years I've discovered **wildlife photography** for myself, and ever since I've been capturing spectacular scenes that often happen right in our immediate surroundings. I've made it my mission not only to photograph the most breathtaking animals and rarest birds, but also the ones we might not consider all that spectacular at first glance.

The **macro world** in particular offers some truly impressive scenes.

Have fun exploring my portfolio and marvelling at nature!


## What I mainly do

**🦅 Wildlife & birds**

What I enjoy most: stalking with the telephoto lens or sitting in wait, bringing everything far away up close.

**🐛 Macro photography**

I'm continually fascinated by this small world we barely perceive, and its sense of scale.
